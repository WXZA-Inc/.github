# WXZA Inc.

**The Sentient Venture OS** — a venture studio in Ottawa, Canada.

WXZA designs, builds and runs digital products across wealth, health, real estate, education, commerce and AI. Founded 2023 by [Loïc Hazoume](https://www.wxza.net/founder-loic-hazoume/), incorporated federally in Canada (CBCA no. 1738920-5).

- **Flagship:** [DomainEx](https://domainex.app) — advertising intelligence for Meta and TikTok ads
- **Creative branch:** Atable Studio — brands, websites and digital products
- **Security:** BEEMAZE — agentic security suite
- **Open layer:** AYI, sentient-OS and the Lattice
